## Global Constraints

- Banked values come from sources read during the build, never from memory.
  - The notes count as a source for what they print. Primary papers are fetched, and one that cannot be fetched is reported.
  - Each bank key has `value`, `tol`, `cite` (with equation, table, figure or section) and `independent_route`.
  - A value read off a figure says so, and its tolerance covers the reading error. A printed value that looks truncated is banked with a tolerance of one unit in its last digit.
  - Numbers quoted in this plan are pointers to verify, never targets to type in.
- The notes' errata (spec, Sources) are never banked as printed. The corrected form and the route that confirms it go in the bank `cite` or the GUIDE.
- Conventions are mapped explicitly: λ_notes = λ_LM = λ_replicant², α_notes = 1/γ_ridge, β_Potts = −2 β_Ising for the ferromagnet. Each entry states its own convention in its docstring.
- A banked number is a target, never an input of the computation it checks.
- Every check ships a positive control: a deliberately wrong variant that must fail. Planted errors go through module `_HOOKS`, restored in `try/finally` with identity asserts. Statistical checks state their power against the plant and are sized for power ≥ 0.9.
- Refuse rather than invent:
  - a named `Refusal(reason, detail)`, never a silent fallback;
  - unknown run parameters are refused;
  - each entry and route declares its parameter set.
- A correct algorithm is never refused at runtime. When a diagnostic fails (BP or AMP not converged, planted and random starts disagreeing with no free-entropy decision), the affected values are labelled `undetermined` and the rest are kept. An undetermined run never feeds an asserted band.
- Per-value finality as in every series:
  - `exact-by-literature` only with a fetched theorem and its cite;
  - `certified` only for true arb enclosures;
  - finite-N runs are statistical: stderr plus a 99% Student-t band over named seeds, never final;
  - a Bethe free entropy on a loopy graph is labelled an approximation.
- No new route name without a recorded ruling. The existing routes (`replica`, `cavity`, `amp`, `exact`, `series`, `bound`, `online`, `sample`, and `perceptron`'s `rsb`) cover the plan.
- mpmath precision only through `mp.workdps`.
- Budget:
  - at most about 1 s of default-tier growth per task, ≲ 7 s for K1–K7;
  - measured as the default selftest growth on a clean export, against a baseline the controller measures cold before K1 and records in the ledger;
  - heavy runs are `@slow`, at most about 2 min per test.
- Files ≲ 400 lines. The guarded files `catalog_entries.py`, `compose.py`, `latex.py` and `latex_entries.py` stay ≤ 400. `catalog.py` is already 411: K1 moves the registry inside `catalog()` into a new `catalog_index.py` before adding entries, with `catalog()`'s behaviour unchanged.
- Docs are in English. Commit messages end with the harness attribution trailer. Nothing under `.superpowers` is committed. Run with `/Users/renatovicente/Projects/bootloops/.venv/bin/python3`.
- Do not change existing entries' numbers or behaviour. Extensions of `sbm`, `spiked_wigner`, `sk`, `rem` and `ridge` add parameters, and the old parameter sets give identical results.

## Review Focus

1. **A generic layer checked only against itself.** `factor/` BP must reproduce `bethe_ising` (q = 2 Potts) and `sbm` (q = 2) through code that shares nothing with them. `linear/` GAMP must reproduce `amp/gamp.py` on the sign channel.
2. **Convention slips between the notes, the primaries and replicant:** λ versus λ², α versus 1/γ, the Potts β, c versus the mean degree, a_in/a_out versus c_in/c_out. Each entry's convention test checks one mapped number in both directions.
3. **First-order transitions.** c_d, c_c and the spinodals need both planted and random BP starts and a Bethe free-entropy comparison. A single converged start is not a transition point.
4. **Bethe on loops.** Enumeration on a small loopy graph differs from Bethe, and the test asserts that difference exists rather than hiding it.
5. **LASSO calibration.** AMP's threshold θ and the LASSO λ are related by a calibration equation the notes do not print. Derive it and check it against the exact convex solver at finite N. It is never fitted.

### Task K1: Mean-field foundations (Ch. 1–3)

**Files:**
- `catalog_index.py` (the moved registry);
- `meanfield/__init__.py`, `meanfield/curie_weiss.py`, `meanfield/rfim.py`;

- `catalog_meanfield.py`, `latex_meanfield.py`, `reference/meanfield.json`;
- `tests/test_curie_weiss.py`, `tests/test_rfim.py`, `tests/test_random_matrix.py`.

**Interfaces:**
- `cw_magnetization(beta, h=0, branch=None) -> Ball | float`: the root of m = tanh(β(m + h)), with every branch listed when β > 1 and h is inside the spinodal.
- `cw_free_entropy(beta, h)`, `cw_spinodal(beta) -> h_sp`.
- `cw_rate_function(m, beta, h)`: by Legendre and by Gärtner–Ellis, as two routes.
- `cw_exact_z(N, beta, h)`: the binomial sum, exact, in balls.
- `rfim_rs(beta, J, field_law) -> RS(m, f, e)` and `rfim_ground_energy(J, field_law)`; `rfim_guerra_bound(...)`.
- `stieltjes_wigner(z)`, `stieltjes_mp(z, alpha)`, `density(kind, x, **p)`, by a replica route and a cavity route kept in separate functions.
- Entries:
  - `curie_weiss`: routes `replica`, `cavity`, `exact`;
  - `rfim`: route `replica` here, `cavity` in K3;
  - `random_matrix`: routes `replica`, `cavity`, with kind `wigner` | `wishart`.

**Tests:**
- `test_registry_move_is_identity`: `catalog()` lists the same 38 entries in the same order before and after the move.
- `test_cw_critical_point_and_spinodal`: β_c = 1. The spinodal closed form against a numerical root. A planted wrong sign of h must fail.
- `test_cw_three_toolboxes_agree`: the variational, cavity and field-theoretic routes give the same free entropy. Each route has its own function, and a test asserts they share no code (as `test_simulator_shares_no_code_with_the_ode` does).
- `test_cw_exact_z_converges`: (1/N) ln Z_N approaches the mean-field free entropy with the O(1/N) correction, and the rate function at finite N matches the Legendre route.
- `test_rfim_rs_vs_exact_ground_state`: the RS ground energy against exact finite-N ground states (sorted fields), as a statistical band. The Guerra bound lies above the RS value.
- `test_spectra_vs_eigvalsh`:
  - the semicircle √(4 − λ²)/(2π) on [−2, 2] and the MP edges (1 ± √α)², as printed in the notes, banked;
  - histograms of `eigvalsh` at N = 400 over named seeds lie within a band;
  - a planted wrong edge must fail.
